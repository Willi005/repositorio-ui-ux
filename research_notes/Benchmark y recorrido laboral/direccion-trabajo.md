# Chile's Labor Directorate: labor guidance and procedure access

Observed **October 5, 2026**. Scope: documentary research of official public content for UX benchmarking. No login, submitted procedures, or real case outcomes were evaluated. Connections with Benjamin, William, and Joseph reflect the project's assigned scenarios; opportunities are design hypotheses, not user test results or legal advice.

## 1. What value and public navigation does DT offer?

### Takeaway

DT combines labor answers organized by topic with formal services and assistance channels. Its documentary architecture supports finding guidance and accessing procedures, but requires distinguishing content, portals, and competent institutions. [Consultation center](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-channel.html), [Assistance channels](https://dt.gob.cl/portal/1626/w3-propertyvalue-23599.html).

### Cited Findings

- The consultation center includes search and categories such as contract termination, compensation, working hours, social security, and migrant workers. Mi DT questions are grouped by procedure, including complaints and electronic settlements. This documents public content, without claiming search effectiveness or ease of use. [Consultation center](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-channel.html).
- Telephone service offers labor consultation and Mi DT information. Contact information directs OIRS requests and questions to SUAC, and procedure tracking to SUAC with ClaveÚnica or in-person help. The published number is 600 450 4000, Monday–Friday, 09:00–17:00. [Contact](https://dt.gob.cl/portal/1626/w3-propertyvalue-23599.html).
- Mi DT access documentation specifies DT website → Mi DT → login → RUN and ClaveÚnica. Its stated update date is December 14, 2023. [Mi DT access](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-116464.html).
- A FAQ lists worker, employer, and union leader profiles, allowing more than one. Updated August 5, 2022; it is documentary evidence, not inspection of the current authenticated interface. [Mi DT profiles](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-116467.html).
- The settlement access FAQ, updated May 31, 2023, mentions Procedures and Services. The current public page documents a different employer route: Employer profile → Employment contracts and dismissal → Electronic employment settlement. Keep these routes distinct; do not attribute the employer route to a worker. [Access FAQ](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-118104.html), [Settlement page](https://dt.gob.cl/portal/1626/w3-article-117245.html).

### Inferences

- DT is a direct functional guidance reference and official action destination. Problem-focused entry could help select relevant content/procedures before directing users to the corresponding portal.
- Coexisting legal categories and procedure names suggest testing everyday entry phrases about unpaid hours, understanding a settlement, or leave problems. Improvements need validation.
- Visible source, date, scope, and destination help distinguish general guidance from formal procedures.

### Gaps

- Interactive search, mobile navigation, full accessibility, performance, and user comprehension were not verified.
- Authenticated onboarding and institutional response times were not observed. Described routes are documented.
- The linked settlement manual was identified, but extraction timed out when reopened; its screens were not used as current observations. [Linked manual](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1626/articles-117245_recurso_2.pdf).
- Three candidate public capture pages: [Consultation center](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-channel.html), [Settlement](https://dt.gob.cl/portal/1626/w3-article-117245.html), and [Interurban freight driver guidance](https://dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-60075.html). These do not represent the complete flow.

## 2. How do William and Joseph relate to procedure preparation?

### Takeaway

Cases require classification before action: William must confirm the applicable working time regime, and Joseph must review a specific settlement proposal and alternatives. Public documentation shows requirements/steps, but does not establish unaided comprehension. [Interurban freight working time](https://dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-60075.html), [Settlement](https://dt.gob.cl/portal/1626/w3-article-117245.html).

### Cited Findings

- Guidance specifically for **interurban** road freight drivers describes Article 25 bis, 180 monthly hours, driving/rest rules, and a change from April 26, 2028. This concerns that particular sector; cargo driver alone is insufficient to assign it automatically to William. [DT guidance](https://dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-60075.html).
- ORD. No. 843, dated December 30, 2025, distinguishes waiting time from auxiliary tasks specified in the contract, with legal and case law references. It illustrates why overtime guidance needs context, a contract, and time classification. [DT ruling](https://dt.gob.cl/legislacion/1624/w3-article-128749.html).
- The labor complaint FAQ, updated April 20, 2026, describes a confidential complaint for a possible inspection concerning labor, pension, health/safety, and fundamental rights/Karin Law violations. [Procedure description](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-126454.html).
- Filing requires Mi DT and ClaveÚnica. Documented minimum evidence includes applicant data, employer data, and complaint details. [Access](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-126456.html), [Evidence](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-126465.html).
- Requirements distinguish current employment and exceptions, court proceedings, and the procedure's time limit. Verify these when preparing referrals; the page is not a universal rule for every complaint. [Published requirements](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-126464.html).
- Complaint confirmation documents an on-screen 12-letter code to save and an email within the next 48 hours. This is stated feedback/continuity, not observed compliance. [Submission notification](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-126458.html).
- In electronic settlements, the employer creates the proposal and the worker may reject, accept with reservation of rights, or accept. The page requires an active worker bank account; otherwise it indicates office service. It describes an in-person alternative and employer pension requirements. In-person information for foreign nationals includes a passport or origin-country identification. [DT settlement](https://dt.gob.cl/portal/1626/w3-article-117245.html).
- The settlement FAQ states ten working days, Monday–Saturday, to accept with/without reservation or reject the received proposal. Recorded as a requirement of the reviewed service, not a recommendation about which decision to make. [Published response period](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-118108.html).

### Inferences

- **William:** ask transport type, interurban/urban operation, agreed hours, and available records before showing a rule. An unfiltered generic overtime calculator could guide users toward an incorrect rule.
- **Joseph:** UX value lies in explaining the document, data to check, and available alternatives, with clear language and human help. Do not assume RUN/ClaveÚnica, an enabled account, or electronic eligibility for every settlement.
- An evidence summary and official links could reduce context switching. A saved checklist and tracking-code reminder are continuity hypotheses derived from documented steps.

### Gaps

- William's regime cannot be determined from his generic occupation. His contract/attendance records were not reviewed.
- Joseph's eligibility and settlement amount were not verified. Acceptance, reservation, and rejection were neither performed nor simulated.
- Complaint confirmation was not observed. Email arrival within the stated period was not verified.

## 3. What source verification and referral does Benjamin need?

### Takeaway

DT provides medical leave guidance, but its FAQ refers appeals to COMPIN and SUSESO. Differences between the DT summary and SUSESO compendium concerning time limits and situations require verifying authority, rejection type, and process stage before presenting instructions. [DT FAQ](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-95287.html), [SUSESO compendium](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/622/w3-propertyvalue-788701.html).

### Cited Findings

- The consultation center includes medical leave questions about rejection, benefits, submission to an employer, and appeals. [DT medical leave](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-propertyvalue-27203.html).
- The appeal FAQ, updated March 14, 2025, identifies COMPIN then SUSESO and links both. Its wording generalizes fifteen working days for Fonasa/Isapre insured people. [DT FAQ](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-95287.html).
- The SUSESO compendium distinguishes Isapre rejection/modification and a complaint before COMPIN. For Fonasa, it describes reconsideration before the COMPIN issuing the decision, within five days of notification. It also covers general complaints before SUSESO concerning COMPIN decisions. The DT summary therefore does not establish a single period for both situations. [SUSESO compendium](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/622/w3-propertyvalue-788701.html).
- SUSESO assistance also distinguishes incapacity benefit complaints and rejection reasons. Problem type matters when selecting destination/evidence. [SUSESO complaints](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-567278.html).

### Inferences

- **Benjamin:** medical leave problem is an insufficient classifier. Distinguish employer processing, authorization status, rejection/reduction, and benefit payment; ask institution, reason, and received decision; route to the appropriate authority.
- Explain referral: why the institution is recommended, what to prepare, the supporting source, and review date. Avoid a generic DT complaint instruction for every leave issue.
- Official source discrepancies demonstrate the need for content review and traceability. Do not declare DT globally outdated or legally decide a case from this comparison.

### Gaps

- Benjamin's affiliation, reason, status, and decision are unknown; no individual procedure or deadline can be stated.
- This research covers common-origin medical leave reflected in the sources. An occupational/common-origin dispute requires separate classification and verification.
- Deadline discrepancies were documented; no institutional ruling was requested and no legal case analysis was performed. The Journey should express the need to verify rather than turn a website summary into an individual decision.
