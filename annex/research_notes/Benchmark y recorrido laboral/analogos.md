# Analogues and references: SUSESO and ChileAtiende

Observed October 5, 2026. Documentary research using official sources and public content. No accounts were accessed and no procedures submitted. Inferences are project proposals, not user test results. Team-provided personas: Benjamin, 24, retail, Valdivia, first medical leave; William, 51, transport, Temuco, overtime; Joseph, 42, warehouse worker, Concepción, settlement after eight years.

## What does SUSESO contribute to guidance and tracking?

### Takeaway

SUSESO combines profile-based guidance, evidence preparation, and social security complaint tracking. It provides patterns for preparing users before procedures, with explicit limits on estimates. [Users](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-610.html), [Guidance](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyname-509.html), [SIL simulator](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-article-711911.html).

### Cited Findings

- **Platform and version:** public institutional website linking to PAE; no version number appears on reviewed pages. Observed PAE access redirects to `pae.suseso.gob.cl`. [Users](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-610.html), [PAE login](https://pae.suseso.gob.cl/pae-web/loginCiudadano).
- **Profile:** guidance entries cover workers, families, complaints against a benefits fund, and certificates/records/forms/decisions. The worker entry describes people making regular contributions with an employer. [Guidance](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyname-509.html), [Worker guidance](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-34955.html).
- **Value and authority:** decides appeals and complaints about social security rights involving supervised entities. Its mandate is defined by subjects and institutions. [What we do](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/601/w3-propertyvalue-10381.html), [Supervised entities](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/609/w3-propertyname-535.html).
- **Public features:** complaints, tracking, incapacity benefit (SIL) simulation, online procedures, guidance, supplementary medical reports, and educational material. [Users](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-610.html).
- **Navigation:** one page separates filing, checking progress, and preparing a complaint. Worker guidance classifies problems such as medical leave, accidents, and SIL; detail pages provide recommendations, requirements, processing information, and help. [Complaint information](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-article-689771.html), [Worker guidance](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-34955.html).
- **Visual observation supplied by the coordinator:** the user page has separate complaint and tracking buttons, and the guidance tool presents profiles. The medical leave screen offers rejection/modification or absence of a COMPIN decision; no inline explanation of the formal decision wording was visible. At 866 × 922, text size controls partly covered Back. This is a specific observation, not an overall accessibility conclusion. [Users](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-610.html), [Guidance](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyname-509.html), [Medical leave](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-586.html).
- **Observed onboarding:** guidance and preparation are publicly readable. PAE offers ClaveÚnica, tax credentials, and password access reserved for legal entities. The authenticated flow was not tested. [PAE login](https://pae.suseso.gob.cl/pae-web/loginCiudadano).
- **Evidence preparation:** the unjustified rest complaint page shows examples of a COMPIN decision, medical report, and paper medical leave; it requires prior reconsideration before COMPIN. [File a complaint](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-article-40310.html).
- **Tracking and uploads:** the tutorial describes submission, evidence, assessment, and decision; the summary must be completed before submission. It documents PDF/JPG, up to 120 MB per file, an OK confirmation, and magnifier review. Case status is checked through Mi Portal with ClaveÚnica/RUN. Delay depends on complexity; the page declares high demand. [Complaint lifecycle](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-562466.html).
- **Simulator and limits:** for common medical leave of private dependent workers, it requests the last three payslips and Isapre plan cost where applicable. It states that results are estimates and excludes prior/consecutive leave. [SIL introduction](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-article-711911.html). The public form includes taxable income, days worked, occasional compensation, and amount validation; no data was entered. [SIL form](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-article-712302.html).
- **Referral:** the compendium requires prior complaint stages before COMPIN to be exhausted before approaching SUSESO about rejected/reduced medical leave. [Compendium](https://www.suseso.cl/622/w3-propertyvalue-788701.html).

### Inferences

- For Benjamin, separating understanding a first leave, estimating payment, and challenging a decision avoids turning an initial question into a premature complaint. Explain why each item of evidence is needed and which institution comes next, using simulator limits and COMPIN referral. Do not promise exact payment. [SIL introduction](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-article-711911.html), [Compendium](https://www.suseso.cl/622/w3-propertyvalue-788701.html).
- For William and Joseph, retain SUSESO as a tracking pattern reference without presenting overtime or severance as SIL subjects. Observed features concern social security. [Users](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-610.html).
- Evidence review before confirmation and states explained in everyday language are transferable. These patterns are supported by documentary preparation and the published lifecycle. [Complaint](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-article-40310.html), [Lifecycle](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-562466.html).

### Gaps

- The tutorial mentions creating a password without ClaveÚnica; the current login screen reserves password access for legal entities. Do not assume a worker can now create an alternative personal account. [Tutorial](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-562466.html), [Current screen](https://pae.suseso.gob.cl/pae-web/loginCiudadano).
- Extracted simulator HTML shows months in 2023. This may be initial content changed by JavaScript; visual observation is needed before concluding the interface uses old dates. [Form](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-article-712302.html).
- Mobile usability, contrast, task time, calculation success, real uploads, and authenticated tracking screens were not verified. Text extraction cannot establish them.
- Three candidate screens: [Users](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-610.html), [Guidance](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyname-509.html), [SIL introduction](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-article-711911.html). Public transactional alternative: [PAE login](https://pae.suseso.gob.cl/pae-web/loginCiudadano).

## What does ChileAtiende contribute to information by need and channel?

### Takeaway

ChileAtiende offers access to multiple services with requirements, steps, and channels, organized by topic and life event. It is especially relevant to guiding Joseph from the event of losing his job. [About](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/que-es-chileatiende), [Life events](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/momentos-de-vida).

### Cited Findings

- **Platform, version, and profile:** IPS-managed institutional website for people seeking state benefits/services; no version number obtained. Its proposition is simple information about how/where to complete procedures and their requirements. [About](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/que-es-chileatiende).
- **Access and features:** information pages are public. Help describes recipient profiles, requirements, documents, steps, cost, validity, and legal framework. The portal and guidance are free, although a specific procedure may cost money. [Help center](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/ayuda/centro-de-ayuda).
- **Need-based navigation:** life events include losing work, public healthcare, having a child, housing, and retirement. Work/unemployment lists services such as settlement and dismissal complaints. [Life events](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/momentos-de-vida), [Work and unemployment](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/temas/trabajo-y-cesantia).
- **Channel navigation:** the network distinguishes in-person and remote service, including branches, modules, appointments, video, 101, and written forms. Availability of every procedure across all channels was not verified. [Service network](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/red-de-atencion).
- **Benjamin's leave:** the Fonasa page describes processing, status, and reconsideration, identifies COMPIN as the information provider, and warns before redirecting to its website. Status uses ClaveÚnica or RUT/case number at the destination. Downloadable `.ics` reminders or saving with ClaveÚnica are also documented but were not executed. [Medical leave page](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/53052-estado-de-una-licencia-medica-solo-asegurados-de-fonasa).
- **Joseph's settlement:** sections separate obtaining, ratifying, and requesting a copy; they refer to DT/Inspectorate or a notary. Online ratification requires ClaveÚnica at DT; free Inspectorate advice is stated. [Settlement](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/33522).
- **Event-based route:** dismissal guidance proposes reviewing before ratification, refers inconsistencies to DT or the Legal Aid Corporation, and connects settlement with income, health, and job search. [Facing dismissal](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/momentos-de-vida/Quedar%2Bsin%2Btrabajo/Revisa%2Bqu%C3%A9%2Bhacer%2Bsi%2Bquedaste%2Bsin%2Btrabajo).
- **Visual observation supplied by the coordinator:** the settlement page opened without login and displayed January 27, 2026 as update date, explained grounds as reason, linked advice, and showed accordions and a fixed Ratify settlement action. Expanding Ratify revealed Mi DT/ClaveÚnica, offices, and notary instructions. Network cards use a different header, color, and typography. Captures were CA01 (header/action), CA02 (expanded ratification), and CA03 (network). [Settlement](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/33522), [Network](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/red-de-atencion).
- **Authority and referral:** pages identify the information provider; the portal explains benefits and connects users to responsible channels/institutions. Do not infer that ChileAtiende decides labor cases or provides individualized severance calculations. [Leave page](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/53052-estado-de-una-licencia-medica-solo-asegurados-de-fonasa), [About](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/que-es-chileatiende).

### Inferences

- For Benjamin, start with first medical leave, ask for context needed to route appropriately, and distinguish public guidance from COMPIN status checks. Avoid RUT or medical documents for a general explanation. [Leave page](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/53052-estado-de-una-licencia-medica-solo-asegurados-de-fonasa).
- For Joseph, a review checklist before signing and explicit referral retain the sequential action pattern of the information page and dismissal route. Eight years of employment alone cannot establish an amount; gather relevant evidence before estimating. [Settlement](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/33522), [Facing dismissal](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/momentos-de-vida/Quedar%2Bsin%2Btrabajo/Revisa%2Bqu%C3%A9%2Bhacer%2Bsi%2Bquedaste%2Bsin%2Btrabajo).
- For William, transfer need-based language and human assistance. Observed navigation does not establish a freight driver overtime calculator; use DT as a supplementary subject reference. [Network](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/red-de-atencion), [DT access](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-116464.html).

### Gaps

- Direct page fetching returned 403; the `portal` domain returned 502/unavailable. Indexed official content was supplemented by coordinator observation/screenshots of the settlement page and network. Procedures, calendars, reminders, and submitted forms were not tested.
- The About page lists 101 service Monday–Friday, 08:00–18:00; some information pages list Monday–Thursday until 20:00. Do not consolidate hours without verifying the current channel and page. [About](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/que-es-chileatiende), [Settlement](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/33522).
- No labor guidance upload form within ChileAtiende was verified. Medical leave evidence is submitted at the destination institution according to the page; do not attribute document analysis to the information portal. [Leave page](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/53052-estado-de-una-licencia-medica-solo-asegurados-de-fonasa).
- Three candidate screens: [Work and unemployment](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/temas/trabajo-y-cesantia), [Settlement](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/fichas/33522), [Network](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/red-de-atencion). Event-focused alternative: [Life events](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/momentos-de-vida).

## How should the ecosystem and selection be classified?

### Takeaway

Distinguish official labor guidance, social security procedures, public information patterns, legal sources, and conversational substitutes. Alternatives should not be scored as though they fulfill the same task.

### Cited Findings

| Initial candidate | Proposed classification | Verified evidence and methodological decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dirección del Trabajo / Mi DT | Functional labor guidance/procedure alternative | Consultation topics; workers use RUN/ClaveÚnica for Mi DT. Include as a close problem reference. [DT](https://www.dt.gob.cl/portal/1628/w3-article-116464.html). |
| SUSESO | Sector preparation/tracking analogue | Social security guidance/complaints, SIL, and tracking. Include for Benjamin's leave and process patterns; not a severance/overtime calculator. [Users](https://www.suseso.gob.cl/606/w3-propertyvalue-610.html). |
| ChileAtiende | Information design/referral reference | Multiple services/channels. Include for needs and channels; information is not an official case decision. [About](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/que-es-chileatiende), [Network](https://www.chileatiende.gob.cl/red-de-atencion). |
| BCN / Ley Chile | Supplementary legal source | Official result describes free access to updated legislation/full text. Exclude from the main guidance/procedure matrix because of documentary focus; retain for source validation. Fetching did not expose the search interface, so no UX behavior is asserted. [Ley Chile](https://www.bcn.cl/leychile/Consulta/). |
| ChatGPT | General conversational substitute | OpenAI acknowledges errors, invented references, and verification needs; documents search with citations. Useful for conversation/trust comparison, without institutional labor authority. [OpenAI](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8313428-does-chatgpt-tell-the-truth). |

### Inferences

- Select DT as functional comparison, SUSESO as sector analogue, and ChileAtiende as design reference. Recognize ChatGPT as a behavioral substitute and BCN as corroboration. This is a study classification, not a market claim; the main matrix contains the three institutional experiences.
- The opportunity combines everyday language, visible official sources, minimal questions, a checklist, and contextual referral. Benefits/usability remain to be validated against the three personas. Reference patterns do not establish demand or product success.

### Gaps

- This is not a complete provider census and cannot establish leadership, market share, or absence of private competitors. Commercial advice services and authenticated accounts were not compared.
- Tasks were not measured and users were not interviewed. Recommendations are design hypotheses grounded in project scenarios and documented public features.
